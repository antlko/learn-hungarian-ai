# Nouns — the plural, the accusative, and stem changes

## The plural `-k`
<!-- topics: plural -->
The plural marker is `-k`. After a vowel it attaches directly, and a final short `a`/`e`
lengthens: `autó` → `autók`, `alma` → `almák`, `körte` → `körték`, `kapu` → `kapuk`. After a
consonant a linking vowel is inserted, chosen by harmony and by the stem:

| stem | plural | linking vowel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pad` "bench" | `padok` | `-o-` (back, default) |
| `ház` "house" | `házak` | `-a-` (back, low-vowel stem) |
| `kert` "garden" | `kertek` | `-e-` (front) |
| `föld` "earth" | `földek` | `-e-` (front rounded, most stems) |
| `gyümölcs` "fruit" | `gyümölcsök` | `-ö-` (front rounded, some stems) |
| `könyv` "book" | `könyvek` | `-e-` |

The plural is **not** used after numerals or quantifiers: `két ház` "two houses", `sok ember`
"many people", `néhány könyv` "a few books", `hány gyerek?` "how many children?". The verb
agrees with that singular: `Két fiú jön.` "Two boys are coming."

## The accusative `-t`
<!-- topics: accusative -->
The direct object takes `-t`. After a vowel it attaches directly, and a final short `a/e`
lengthens: `autót`, `almát`, `körtét`, `kaput`. After the consonants `j, l, ly, n, ny, r, s,
sz, z, zs` it also attaches directly: `asztalt` "table", `tanárt` "teacher", `lányt` "girl",
`gázt`, `buszt`, `pénzt` "money". After every other consonant a linking vowel is inserted:
`padot`, `kertet`, `gyümölcsöt`, `könyvet`, `vonatot` "train", `kalapot` "hat". Two groups of stems take a linking vowel even after a "direct" consonant:
the low-vowel stems (`ház` → `házat`, `toll` → `tollat`, `hal` → `halat`) and the shortening
stems (`víz` → `vizet`, `kéz` → `kezet`, `nyár` → `nyarat`).

The same stems take `-a-/-e-` in the plural and in the accusative: a word that has `házak` has
`házat`; `kertek` goes with `kertet`. Adjectives used as nouns follow adjective rules:
`nagyot`, `pirosat`, `magasat`.

Some pronouns and short words have irregular objects: `ki` → `kit`, `mi` → `mit`, `ez` → `ezt`,
`az` → `azt`, `én` → `engem`, `te` → `téged`, `ő` → `őt`, `mi` → `minket`, `ti` → `titeket`,
`ők` → `őket`.

## Stems that lengthen and stems that shorten
<!-- topics: stem-alternation -->
**Final `a`/`e` lengthen before almost every suffix**: `alma` → `almát, almák, almában,
almám, almáig`; `fekete` → `feketét, feketék, feketében`. The suffixes that do **not** lengthen
a final `a/e` are `-i` (`Buda` → `budai`), `-kor` (`óra` → `órakor` "at … o'clock"), `-ként`
(`példa` → `példaként` "as an example"), `-szor/-szer/-ször`, `-ság/-ség` (`tiszta` →
`tisztaság`), `-féle`, `-beli` and `-szerű`.

**Shortening stems**: a long `á` or `é` in the last syllable becomes short in about sixty
common nouns before `-k`, `-t`, the possessive suffixes and `-s`:
`nyár` "summer" → `nyarat, nyarak, nyaram`; `kéz` "hand" → `kezet, kezek, kezem`;
`víz` "water" → `vizet, vizek`; `tűz` "fire" → `tüzet`; `út` "road" → `utat, utak`;
`híd` → `hidat`; `tél` "winter" → `telet`; `madár` "bird" → `madarat, madarak`;
`egér` "mouse" → `egeret`; `levél` "letter/leaf" → `levelet`; `kenyér` "bread" → `kenyeret`;
`szél` "wind" → `szelet`; `név` "name" → `nevet, nevem`; `ég` "sky" → `eget`.
The long vowel stays before `-ban`, `-ból`, `-hoz` etc.: `nyárban`, `kézben`, `vízben`.

## Fleeting vowels
<!-- topics: stem-alternation -->
In many two-syllable stems the last vowel drops before a vowel-initial suffix:
`bokor` "bush" → `bokrot, bokrok`; `tükör` "mirror" → `tükröt, tükrök`; `terem` "hall" →
`termet`; `álom` "dream" → `álmot, álmom`; `torony` "tower" → `tornyot`; `dolog` "thing" →
`dolgot, dolgok`; `ökör` → `ökröt`; `majom` "monkey" → `majmot`; `sarok` "corner" → `sarkot`;
`ezer` "thousand" → `ezret`. The vowel returns before a consonant-initial suffix:
`bokorban`, `teremben`, `dologról`.

## `v`-stems
<!-- topics: stem-alternation -->
A small group ends in a long vowel in the dictionary form and shows `v` before vowel-initial
suffixes: `ló` "horse" → `lovat, lovak, lovam`; `kő` "stone" → `követ, kövek`; `tó` "lake" →
`tavat, tavak`; `hó` "snow" → `havat`; `fű` "grass" → `füvet`; `fő` "head/chief" → `fejet`
(irregular); `szó` "word" → `szót` (regular!) but `szavak` "words", `szavam`; `mű` "work" →
`művet`; `bő` → `bővebb`; `falu` "village" → `falut` but `falvak` (plural). Before consonant
suffixes the dictionary stem stays: `lóval`, `kőből`, `tóban`.

## Words whose final vowel alternates
<!-- topics: stem-alternation -->
`ajtó` "door" → `ajtaja` "its door", `ajtót`; `idő` "time" → `ideje`; `mező` → `mezeje`;
`erdő` "forest" → `erdeje`; `apa` → `apja`; `fiú` "boy/son" → `fia` "his son" but `fiúja` "her
boyfriend"; `anya` → `anyja`. These are possessive-specific and are listed in the pack's
third-person possessive appendix.

## Where speakers of Russian go wrong
<!-- topics: plural, accusative -->
- Putting the noun in the plural after a numeral: `három könyv`, not `három könyvek`.
- Forgetting the accusative on a definite object because Russian marks animacy differently:
  every direct object takes `-t` — `Látom a házat`, `Ismerem Pétert`.
- Not shortening: `nyárat` is wrong, `nyarat` is right; `kézet` is wrong, `kezet` right.
- Lengthening the linking vowel: `házát` is "his house" (possessive + accusative), `házat` is
  simply "house" in the accusative.

### Other symbols
A stress mark is placed before the syllable that is stressed.

| IPA | indicates |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | primary stress |
| (a) | secondary stress |
| / / | phonemes, as in /ˈinformɒtikɒ/ |
| [ ] | speech sounds, as in [ˈiɱformɒtikɒ] |

# The instrumental, the remaining cases, postpositions and time

## The instrumental `-val/-vel` and its assimilation
<!-- topics: instrumental -->
`-val/-vel` means "with" (accompaniment and instrument) and "by" (means of transport):
`Péterrel` "with Péter", `késsel` "with a knife", `busszal` "by bus", `autóval` "by car",
`a barátommal` "with my friend". After a consonant the `v` **assimilates** to that consonant,
which is then written double: `kés` + `-vel` → `késsel`; `busz` + `-val` → `busszal`; `ház` →
`házzal`; `Péter` → `Péterrel`; `könyv` → `könyvvel` (the `v` is already there, so `vv`);
`gyerek` → `gyerekkel`; `tej` → `tejjel`; `lány` → `lánnyal` (digraph: `ny+ny` → `nny`);
`gulyás` → `gulyással`. After a vowel the `v` stays and a final `a/e` lengthens: `autóval`,
`almával`, `Annával`, `Imrével`.

Pronouns: `velem, veled, vele, velünk, veletek, velük`. Common verbs with `-val/-vel`:
`beszél valakivel` "talks with", `találkozik valakivel` "meets", `foglalkozik valamivel`
"deals with", `egyetért valakivel` "agrees with", `tele van valamivel` "is full of".

The translative `-vá/-vé` "into (becoming)" assimilates the same way: `orvossá lett` "became a
doctor", `vízzé válik` "turns into water", `kékké` "(turned) blue".

## The other cases
<!-- topics: remaining-cases -->
| case | suffix | meaning | examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| causal-final | `-ért` | for (the sake of, in exchange for) | `a pénzért` "for the money", `érted` "for you", `miért?` "why?", `Elmegyek kenyérért.` "I'm going for bread." |
| terminative | `-ig` | as far as, until | `a házig` "up to the house", `estig` "until evening", `két óráig` "for two hours / until two o'clock", `meddig?` "how far / how long?" |
| translative | `-vá/-vé` | into (a change of state) | `tanárrá vált` "became a teacher", `porrá` "to dust" |
| temporal | `-kor` | at (a point in time) | `ötkor` "at five", `karácsonykor` "at Christmas", `mikor?` "when?", `érkezéskor` "on arrival" |
| essive-formal | `-ként` | as, in the capacity of | `tanárként dolgozik` "works as a teacher", `példaként` "as an example" |
| essive-modal | `-ul/-ül` | as, in (a language); adverb former | `magyarul` "in Hungarian", `feleségül vesz` "takes as wife", `rosszul` "badly" |
| distributive | `-nként` | per, each | `naponként` "daily", `fejenként` "per head", `óránként` "hourly" |
| sociative | `-stul/-stül` | together with | `családostul` "with the whole family", `mindenestül` "with everything" |
| distributive-temporal | `-nta/-nte` | every | `naponta` "every day", `hetente` "weekly", `esténként` "in the evenings" |
| locative (old) | `-t/-tt` | at (a few town names) | `Győrött`, `Pécsett`, `Vácott` |

Neither `-ért`, `-ig`, `-kor` nor `-ként` harmonises. `-ért` and `-ig` lengthen a final `a/e`
(`Budáért`, `Budáig`); `-kor` and `-ként` do not (`órakor`, `példaként`).

## Postpositions
<!-- topics: remaining-cases, locative-grid -->
Postpositions follow the noun, which stays in the nominative. Most have three forms — where,
where to, where from — like the locative cases:

| where | where to | where from | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `alatt` | `alá` | `alól` | under |
| `fölött / felett` | `fölé` | `fölül` | above |
| `előtt` | `elé` | `elől` | in front of; before (time) |
| `mögött` | `mögé` | `mögül` | behind |
| `mellett` | `mellé` | `mellől` | beside |
| `között` | `közé` | `közül` | between, among |
| `körül` | `köré` | — | around |
| `után` | — | — | after |

`A macska az asztal alatt van.` "The cat is under the table." `A macska az asztal alá bújt.`
"The cat crept under the table." `Kijött az asztal alól.` "It came out from under the table."
`az óra után` "after the lesson", `ebéd előtt` "before lunch", `a két ház között`.

Other common postpositions with the nominative: `óta` "since" (`hétfő óta`), `múlva` "in …
(future)" (`két hét múlva` "in two weeks"), `helyett` "instead of", `nélkül` "without", `miatt`
"because of", `szerint` "according to", `ellen` "against", `iránt` "towards (a feeling)",
`végett` "for the purpose of", `felé` "towards (a direction)", `alatt` also "during" (`egy óra
alatt` "within an hour").

Some postpositions govern a case: `-n keresztül / át` "through, across" (`az erdőn át`), `-n
túl` "beyond" (`a folyón túl`), `-n belül` "within" (`egy héten belül`), `-n kívül` "outside,
apart from", `-hoz képest` "compared with" (`hozzám képest`), `-val együtt` "together with",
`-val szemben` "opposite" (`a bankkal szemben`), `-tól fogva` "from … onwards".

Personal forms take the possessive endings: `mellettem` "beside me", `előtted`, `mögötte`,
`utánunk`, `köztetek`, `nélkülük` "without them", `miattam` "because of me", `szerintem` "in
my opinion".

## Time expressions
<!-- topics: remaining-cases -->
- Days: `-n`: `hétfőn`, `szerdán`, `szombaton`, `vasárnap` (no suffix!).
- Parts of the day: `reggel`, `délelőtt`, `délben`, `délután`, `este`, `éjjel` (no suffix, or
  `-kor` for a specific occasion).
- Months and years: `-ban/-ben`: `májusban`, `1999-ben`; `idén` "this year", `tavaly` "last
  year", `jövőre` "next year".
- Clock time: `-kor`: `nyolckor` "at eight", `fél kilenckor` "at half past eight" (literally
  "half nine"), `negyed tízkor` "at quarter past nine" ("quarter ten"), `háromnegyed tízkor`
  "at quarter to ten".
- Duration: accusative or `-ig`: `két órát vártam` / `két óráig vártam` "I waited two hours";
  `egy hétre` "for a week (planned)".
- Frequency: `naponta`, `hetente`, `havonta`, `évente`; `kétszer egy héten` "twice a week".
- "ago": `ezelőtt` after the accusative-less noun with `-val/-vel`: `két évvel ezelőtt` "two
  years ago"; "in (future)": `két év múlva`; "since": `2010 óta`.

## Where speakers of Russian go wrong
<!-- topics: instrumental, remaining-cases -->
- Writing `-val` after a consonant without assimilating: `Péterval` is wrong; `Péterrel`.
- Putting the noun in a case before a postposition: `a házban mellett` is wrong; `a ház
  mellett`.
- `vasárnapon`: Sunday takes no suffix — `vasárnap`.
- Reading `fél kilenc` as "half past nine": it is 8:30.
